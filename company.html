Company Profile & About Us

A Trusted Partner for Maritime Industrial Lighting

NauticaLight (AVELA) is a leading provider of maritime lights and ship lighting built to high marine industry standards for safety and operational needs at sea.

Why Choose Our Maritime Lighting Products?

With decades of combined experience in marine lighting engineering, we design and supply lighting solutions that withstand the harshest maritime environments — from saltwater corrosion to extreme vibration and temperature variations.

High-Grade Anti-Corrosion Material

Marine-grade aluminum and stainless steel housing rated for saltwater exposure.

Energy Efficient & Vibration Resistant

LED technology with low power consumption and shock-resistant construction for rough seas.

Technical Support & Full Warranty

Dedicated engineering support and comprehensive warranty on all lighting products.

Company Vision

To be the most trusted maritime lighting partner across Indonesia and Southeast Asia, delivering safety and reliability at sea.

Core Mission

To provide high-quality, energy-efficient marine lighting solutions that meet international safety standards for every vessel.

Certification & Standards

All products comply with IMO, USCG, and marine industry standards with full test certificates and documentation.

Our Journey

Company History

A brief story of how AVELA grew to become one of the trusted names in Indonesia's maritime lighting industry.

Early Days

Foundation at Tanjung Priok

AVELA was established in the heart of Tanjung Priok Port, North Jakarta. Starting with a small trading operation, we focused solely on supplying quality marine lighting to local shipyards and vessel owners.

"Starting from a port-side warehouse, with one goal: to provide reliable lighting solutions for Indonesian vessels."

Growth

Expanding to Meet Demand

As demand grew, we expanded our product range beyond basic deck lights to include navigation lights, signal lights, searchlights and floodlights. Our network also grew to serve ship repair yards across Jakarta and surrounding areas.

Building trust one project at a time, by focusing on product quality and after-sales support.

Now

A Trusted Maritime Lighting Partner

Today, AVELA continues to serve commercial vessels, offshore platforms, shipyards, and port facilities across Indonesia. We focus on supplying IMO-compliant lighting, providing technical assistance, and ensuring our clients get the right solution with complete documentation.

Still rooted at Tanjung Priok, now ready to support projects beyond Indonesia with the same commitment to safety and reliability.
